IMPORTANT:
The execution times produced while running under Nsight Compute were
not used for the final benchmark table. Nsight Compute instruments and
replays CUDA kernels while collecting profiling metrics, which adds
substantial profiling overhead.

The final CUDA timing results were therefore obtained from normal
./matrix_mul executions without ncu instrumentation.


============================================================
13. RELEVANT ISSUES / ERRORS
============================================================

During analysis, the timings produced under NVIDIA Nsight Compute were
initially compared with normal execution timings. The profiler output
showed substantially larger kernel times because ncu performed multiple
profiling passes.

The issue was resolved by separating profiling from benchmarking:
- ncu output was retained for profiling information.
- Normal ./matrix_mul executions were used for performance measurements.
- The normal benchmark was repeated three times.
- Arithmetic means were used for the final reported CUDA results.
